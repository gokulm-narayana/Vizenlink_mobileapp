# Scenario: VMS — Access-Controlled, Expiring Share Links

Covers FEAT-199: media share-links generated by VMS must be scoped, time-limited, and
access-audited — never a permanent, unauthenticated direct URL to the raw file.

## Scenario: Creating a time-limited share link for a specific clip

**Scenario ID:** SCN-646
**Feature ID:** FEAT-199

**Persona:** Raj wants to share a short clip with a resident who reported a package theft,
without giving them ongoing camera access.

1. Raj selects the clip in VMS and chooses "Share via link" instead of a direct download.
2. VMS lets him set an expiration (e.g. 24 hours, 7 days, or a specific date) and requires one —
   there is no "never expires" option.
3. VMS generates a unique link scoped to just that clip (not the camera's live feed or other
   recordings) and shows Raj the link to send however he likes.
4. The resident opens the link and can view/download just that clip, with no login to the full
   VMS required.

**What the user expects:** he can share a specific piece of footage without exposing anything
else, and without creating a link that lives forever if he forgets about it.

> **Review:** ⏳ Pending

### Derived Requirements

- **[vms]** The VMS shall require an expiration to be set when creating a share link — there
  shall be no option to create a non-expiring link.
- **[vms]** A share link shall be scoped to exactly the selected clip/time-range, and shall not
  grant access to any other recording, live feed, or camera.
- **[vms]** A share link shall not require the recipient to have or create a VMS account to view
  the scoped content.

## Scenario: The share link expires while the resident still has it open

**Scenario ID:** SCN-647
**Feature ID:** FEAT-199

**Persona:** The resident from SCN-646 tries to reopen the link a week after Raj set a 24-hour
expiration.

1. The resident's browser shows a clear "This link has expired" message — not a broken page, and
   not the raw content served anyway.
2. Raj can generate a fresh link from the same clip if the resident still needs access, rather
   than the original link being extendable after the fact.

**What the user expects:** expiration is a real, enforced boundary, and getting new access after
expiry is a deliberate new action, not an accidental extension of the old link.

> **Review:** ⏳ Pending

### Derived Requirements

- **[vms]** A share link shall stop serving its content once its expiration is reached, showing
  an explicit "expired" state to any subsequent visitor rather than continuing to serve content
  or failing silently.
- **[vms]** An expired share link shall not be extendable in place — only a newly generated link
  can grant renewed access.

## Scenario: Reviewing who has accessed a share link

**Scenario ID:** SCN-648
**Feature ID:** FEAT-199

**Persona:** Raj wants to confirm whether the resident (or anyone else the link may have been
forwarded to) actually viewed the shared clip.

1. Raj opens the share link's detail view in VMS and sees an access log: each time the link was
   opened, with timestamp and, where determinable, requester IP/device info.
2. If the link was opened more times than Raj expected (suggesting it was forwarded beyond the
   intended recipient), he can see that and revoke the link immediately.

**What the user expects:** a share link isn't a black box once it leaves his control — he can
see it was actually used, and cut it off if it's being over-shared.

> **Review:** ⏳ Pending

### Derived Requirements

- **[vms]** The VMS shall log every access to a share link (timestamp, and requester
  IP/device info where available) and show this log to the link's creator.
- **[vms]** The VMS shall let the creator revoke a share link immediately, before its natural
  expiration, at any time.

## Scenario: An operator without export/share permission tries to create a link

**Scenario ID:** SCN-649
**Feature ID:** FEAT-199

**Persona:** A front-desk account with view-only access tries to use the "Share via link"
option.

1. The share option is either unavailable or explicitly denied with a permission message.
2. The attempt is audit-logged, same as an unauthorized export attempt (FEAT-195/FEAT-192).

**What the user expects:** the ability to mint a share link is gated by the same access controls
as any other sensitive-data action, not a lower-friction side door around them.

> **Review:** ⏳ Pending

### Derived Requirements

- **[vms]** Share-link creation shall be gated by the same role/permission scope as footage
  export, and a denied attempt shall be reported to the user and audit-logged.
